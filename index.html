Hearth

A private, self-hosted household app for one family: bills, budgets, savings goals, family dates and reminders, in one place on the family's own computer.

What it is

Hearth runs on a computer in the household's home. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups, and only the members of the one household that runs it can use it. Its assistant proposes; it never moves money or sends messages on anyone's behalf.

How it uses Google

When the household connects a Google account, Hearth asks for three permissions, all used only for that household's own features:

  • Gmail, read-only, one label: Hearth reads only the messages under one label the household chooses, to find bill statements and payment receipts. It never sends, deletes or changes mail.
  • Google Calendar, read-only: to show events from one family calendar the household picks.
  • Google Drive, files it creates: to store the household's own encrypted backups. It cannot see any other file in Drive.

The details are in the Privacy Policy.
